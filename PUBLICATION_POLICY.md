# Publication and Rights Policy

## Allowed by default (subject to licenses/attribution)
Original project documentation and scripts, hashes/metadata, derived factual offsets/addresses, independently created diagrams, reproducible tests, negative-results ledgers, and links to public source locations.

## Do not publish by default
Proprietary firmware/ROM binaries, copyrighted service-manual scans, full third-party articles/posts, private emails/screenshots, unpublished collaborator notes, private-repository contents, access-controlled downloads obtained contrary to terms, or personal contact information not intentionally public for the relevant purpose.

## Third-party unpublished material
Requires explicit permission defining what may be used privately, summarized, quoted, committed publicly, redistributed, or incorporated into software. If scope is ambiguous, treat it as private.

## Provenance
Every external source used in a public technical claim should have enough metadata for another researcher to identify it.

## Takedown/correction
If a rights-holder or contributor raises a credible concern, pause redistribution of disputed material while provenance/permission is reviewed.
